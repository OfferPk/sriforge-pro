\#\# 1\. Commercial Pain Points & Market Demand

\* \*\*The Problem:\*\* Third-party script compromises (Magecart, polyfill.io) cause catastrophic brand damage, data theft, and regulatory penalties.  
\* \*\*Regulatory Driver:\*\* Under \*\*PCI DSS v4.0\*\* (Requirement 6.4.3 & 11.6.1, effective March 2025), any merchant processing payments online is legally mandated to maintain an inventory of all scripts on checkout pages and verify their cryptographic integrity.  
\* \*\*The Cost of Alternatives:\*\* Commercial client-side security vendors (HUMAN, Akamai Page Integrity, Tala) charge \*\*\$500–\$2,000/month\*\*.  
\* \*\*The SRIForge Solution:\*\* A private, 100% client-side studio and developer CLI available for a one-time purchase or low-cost recurring monitoring tier.
